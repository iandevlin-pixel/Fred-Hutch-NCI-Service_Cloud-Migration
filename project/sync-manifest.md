Records which local documents have been pushed to a team-visible surface, and where. Local markdown is the drafting source of truth; the destination is the team review surface.

**Update 2026-09-14:** Ben and Avi agreed a split: Notion for shared knowledge the team and its LLMs read, Jira for tickets and work items. See the candidate impacts in [../discovery/transcripts/2026-09-14-internal-avi-ben-intro.md](../discovery/transcripts/2026-09-14-internal-avi-ben-intro.md).

**The destination is decided as of 2026-09-17.** The roles are split, not duplicated:

- **Notion** holds the knowledge base: transcripts, meeting notes, agendas, discovery findings, decisions and the RAID log. It is what the team and the team's LLMs read. Internal for the most part.
- **Jira** holds the work: tickets, release items, sprints and build items, in project `FHCCNSMS` on `kicksaw.atlassian.net`. The goal is for Fred Hutch to work in Jira alongside Kicksaw. Level of effort is recorded as T-shirt sizing. **Hours never go in Jira.**
- **Fred Hutch SharePoint** remains the client-owned document share.
- **GitHub** holds this repository for the teammates Ben invites: the private repo `bbold-bb/fred-hutch-nci-migration`, branch `main`. Added 2026-10-01. The "Local only" section of `.gitignore` decides what never goes there.

The `Destination` column below records which of these a given document went to. The conventions in this file are the same regardless of transport.

## Rules

- **Nothing syncs autonomously, in either direction.** Ben calls every push and every pull.
- **No document containing real client data is ever eligible for a push.** Not to Notion, not to Jira, not to SharePoint.
- **Internal documents never reach a client-visible surface.** Anything with an `-internal-` segment in its filename stays off SharePoint, off any client Jira project, and out of any message to Fred Hutch, permanently. **Amended 2026-09-15:** an internal document may go to Kicksaw's own Notion workspace when Ben directs that specific push, because Notion is Kicksaw-only. The page is titled `[Internal]` and carries a handling line. The original rule read "internal documents are never eligible" for any destination.
- Re-pushing an updated document updates the same target page or issue. It never creates a duplicate.
- When a document is pushed, add its destination URL to the local file's header block so the local copy links to its review copy.
- This manifest is the record of which side is current. If a row says the local file is newer, the local file is newer.
- **Project Library documents are the exception to re-pushing. Added 2026-09-29.** A document Ben picks for the Notion [Project Library](https://app.notion.com/p/3ead4b87741781f98506e27dccd37967) becomes live in Notion once published, and the team edits it there. The local file gets a frozen line under its title and is never pushed over the Notion page again. Later changes, including the assistant's on Ben's direction, are made in Notion after a fresh fetch.
- **HTML documents stay in sync, not frozen. Added 2026-10-01.** An HTML visual pushed to Notion is embedded as a file, so it can't be edited there, and Ben views the local copy to make decisions. The local file stays the source and the Notion embed always matches it. After any change to the local file, upload it again, replace the embed on the same Notion page, and update Last pushed. This overrides the Project Library freeze for HTML files.

## Pull

When asked to pull team feedback: fetch the target and its comments, diff against the local file, present what changed and who said it. Nothing merges automatically. Ben decides what comes back into local.

## Content that stays local

Working-status content does not push. That means the "Candidate register impacts" tables in transcripts, anything tracking what Kicksaw has or has not verified internally, and any commentary about the engagement rather than about the client's systems. Substance about the client's systems and what is true about them pushes. Commentary about our own process does not.
