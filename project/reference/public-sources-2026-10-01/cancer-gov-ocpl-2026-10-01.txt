Office of Communications and Public Liaison (OCPL)
The National Cancer Institute (NCI) Office of Communications and Public Liaison (OCPL) provides clear, timely, evidence-based information about cancer to the public, patients, health professionals, and the media.

Resources
Cancer.gov
Cancer.gov offers trusted, up-to-date information on cancer prevention, diagnosis, treatment, survivorship, and clinical trials.
Cancer Information Service (CIS)
CIS connects you with trained information specialists who can answer questions about cancer, including treatment options, clinical trials, and quitting smoking. The service is free and confidential. Contact CIS by phone at 1-800-4-CANCER , through LiveHelp chat , or by email at NCIinfo@nih.gov .
