Who's who: NCI, CIS and Fred Hutch

NCI owns the Cancer Information Service, Fred Hutch runs it, and CIS is the name for both the service and the Fred Hutch team that delivers it. Kicksaw's client is Fred Hutch. Fred Hutch has run a CIS center since 1981 and the whole national service since 2010.

Reading the diagram

"CIS" means two things

NCI's public program, and the Fred Hutch team that runs it. In our documents, "internal to CIS" means Fred Hutch's CIS staff only. Nothing in the knowledge base is published to the public.

Kicksaw works for Fred Hutch

The SOW is with Fred Hutch, not NCI. NCI isn't on our calls. Its rules reach the project through Fred Hutch, mostly through Adrianna. NCI's own contract with Fred Hutch is public: award 75N91025C00008, signed March 2025.

Who decides at Fred Hutch

  • Adrianna runs CIS and is "the primary communication with the government" (session 5). Fred Hutch calls her CIS director, successor to Dawn Sittauer (April 2026). She made the calls to start fresh on data (session 2) and take only active users (session 5), and Mark told her "you have final say" on Kicksaw's access to production data (session 5)
  • Mike is the named product owner (kickoff). The SOW makes the product owner "the single point of approval for all milestone sign-offs"
  • Above Adrianna, Fred Hutch's April 2026 article names Garnet Anderson, PhD, as representing Fred Hutch leadership to NCI on site visits. Who sponsors the project and who Adrianna reports to isn't on record

Not yet confirmed

  • Whether the final contract's reports match NCI's 2024 solicitation, and in what format. Adrianna offered to show which reports they use (session 5)
  • How the VA receives callback data, and where demographic results go
  • What CIS does for NCI's Office of the Director (OD). Oracle has an OD queue and product, and nothing public explains it
  • Who at NCI oversees the contract day to day. The contracting officer's representative isn't public
  • Whether anyone at NCI reads or approves knowledge articles

Checked against public sources

On 2026-10-01 this diagram was checked against NCI's public 2024 solicitation for the contract, the federal award record, the Federal Register, cancer.gov and Fred Hutch's news pages. They confirm the contract and its competition, NCI's managing branch, the channels and Adrianna's role. Nothing public covers Mike Griffin, the cloud team or the rest of the CIS team, so those rest on our calls.
